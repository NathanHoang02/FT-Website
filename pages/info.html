Fashion Tailoring & Alterations

Fashion Tailoring & Alterations is a family-owned business that has been serving the Baton Rouge area for over 20 years. We specialize in custom tailoring and alterations for all types of clothing, including formal wear, casual wear, and work attire. Our experienced team of tailors is dedicated to providing high-quality craftsmanship and exceptional customer service.

Whether you need a simple hem or a complete wardrobe makeover, we are here to help. We take pride in our attention to detail and our ability to create garments that fit perfectly and look great. Visit us today to experience the difference that Fashion Tailoring & Alterations can make for your wardrobe.
